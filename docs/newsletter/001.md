# Ask it twice

## One thing worth doing

The most dangerous AI answer is the confident one. Here's the two-minute habit that catches it: ask the same important question in two fresh chats and compare the answers. Two different confident answers means at least one is made up. The same answer twice, with sources you can click? Now you're standing on something.

It works because AI writes by predicting what looks right, and it has no built-in step that asks whether it is right. Full guide: [why AI makes things up](../fundamentals/why-ai-makes-things-up.md).
